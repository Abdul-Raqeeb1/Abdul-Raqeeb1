### 👨‍💻 About Me

**Full Stack Engineer** & **Creative Developer** based in **Karachi, Pakistan**.  

I specialize in building **modern, responsive, and high-performance** web applications that combine striking visual design with robust backend architecture. I actively bridge the gap between traditional development and the future of coding by integrating **agentic AI loops** and "vibe coding" workflows into my process, allowing for unprecedented development speed without sacrificing quality. 

Whether it’s a sleek banking platform, a data-driven dashboard, or comprehensive 3D/2D digital design assets, I deliver production-ready solutions with pixel-perfect precision.

**What sets me apart:**
- ⚡ **Accelerated Development:** Leveraging AI tools to drastically cut down project timelines (recently completing a full digital dashboard from scratch in just three days).
- 🎨 **Multi-Disciplinary Design:** Expert in modern UI/UX principles, dark-mode aesthetics, as well as structural 2D/3D modeling using AutoCAD and Autodesk Maya.
- 🏗️ **Full-Stack Architecture:** Deep expertise in the MERN stack and Django.
- 📈 **Digital Growth:** Strong foundation in digital marketing, SEO, and e-commerce analytics to ensure products don't just look good, but actually perform.
