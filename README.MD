### 👨‍💻 The Man Behind The Code

I am a Full Stack Engineer and Creative Developer based in Karachi, obsessed with pushing the boundaries of how fast and how beautifully we can build software. I don't just write code; I orchestrate it. 

By integrating **agentic AI loops** and modern "vibe coding" workflows into my daily grind, I turn weeks of development time into days—recently spinning up a full, production-ready digital dashboard from scratch in just 72 hours. 

Beyond the web stack, I have a deep appreciation for structural and visual design, bringing ideas to life through **AutoCAD** and **Autodesk Maya**. 

> **Off the keyboard:** When I’m not optimizing a database or rendering a 3D model, you can usually find me clearing my head with my daily 2km walk or powering through a 30-minute home workout to keep the momentum going.
